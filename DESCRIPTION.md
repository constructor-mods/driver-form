Every AI driver in **Grand Prix**, **Driver Career** and **My Team** gets a form for the weekend. It carries through practice, qualifying and the race, so the running order stops playing out the same every time. Works with 2025 and 2026 cars.

### Form
- A third of the grid is in form, a third is out of form, and the rest are neutral.
- The form lasts the whole weekend, even if you close the game between sessions. A new track gives new forms.
- Your own car is never touched.

### In form
- Quicker over a lap, using the game's own AI pace rather than a power or grip boost.
- Hot spells of 20 to 60 seconds where they find extra time.
- Harder to pass: they start defending from further back.

### Out of form
- Slower over a lap.
- They brake earlier, carry less speed through corners and get on the throttle later.
- Bad patches of 20 to 60 seconds where they lose more time.
- Short mistakes of 3 to 8 seconds where they suddenly drop off the pace.

### Real driver gaps
- Optional: team-mates are as far apart as in real qualifying. Alonso is about 0.35 s quicker than Stroll, Sainz
  about 0.2 s quicker than Albon, Norris and Piastri almost level.
- Uses the 2026 averages, or the 2025 ones when the grid is the 2025 line-up. Other drivers keep the game's pace.
- Only team-mates move apart: the car's speed stays the game's. Works with Form strength Off too.

### Settings
Find these in **ADDON SETTINGS**. Everything you can see on screen is off by default, so out of the box the AI simply behaves differently.

- **Form strength**: Off, Subtle (about 0.8 s a lap either way), Normal (1.5 s), Wild (2.2 s) or Chaos (3.5 s). Normal is the default.
- **Real driver gaps**: team-mates as far apart as in real qualifying. Off by default.
- **Bad patches and mistakes**: on or off.
- **In form / Out of form toasts**: name the drivers to watch after the first lap.
- **Messages: Practice**, **Qualifying** and **Race**: how often notifications can appear, from None to Ultra.
- **Show mistakes**, **struggling**, **on a charge**, **place changes** and **pit stops**: pick the notifications you want.

### Needs
- Swofty Launcher 1.8.36 or newer.
- Offline sessions only, like all modded play.
