# Driver Form

AI drivers have a form for the weekend. Some are flying, some are off the pace, and the form lasts through practice, qualifying and the race.

- In-form drivers are quicker.
- Out-of-form drivers brake earlier, carry less corner speed and get on the throttle later.
- Drivers have bad patches, hot spells and short mistakes while the session runs.

Your own car is never changed. Works offline in Grand Prix, Driver Career and My Team.

## Settings (ADDON SETTINGS)

- **Form strength:** Off, Subtle, Normal, Wild, Chaos.
- **Bad patches and mistakes:** on or off.
- **In form / Out of form toasts:** names who is in and out of form after the first lap. Off by default.
- **Messages: Practice, Qualifying, Race:** how often notifications can show, None to Ultra. Off by default.
- **Show mistakes, struggling, on a charge, place changes, pit stops:** pick which notifications you want.
